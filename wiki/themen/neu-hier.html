R3DACTED in 5 sentences

1️⃣ R3DACTED is a German-international DayZ server on Nasdara (Badlands) (PC only, up to 60 players) – PvPvE: PvE in normal play, PvP only in marked zones and announced PvP events.
2️⃣ Your currency is R3D coins (RC): earn them by looting, quests, hunting, fishing, farming and trading.
3️⃣ The hub is the Green Mountain trading post (safe zone) with traders, garage, quest board and machines.
4️⃣ On top: vehicles, base building, airdrops, a boss and the Season Pass (cosmetics).
5️⃣ Everything is earnable – nothing can be bought with real money.

Deine ersten 30 Minuten

1 · Spawn wählen

Nach dem Start öffnet sich die Spawn-Karte: Küste (Zufall) geht immer, 8 Städte (Cherno, Elektro, Kamyshovo, Solnechny, Orlovets, Nizhnee, Berezino, Svetlojarsk) sind danach 10 Minuten gesperrt.

2 · Überleben

Wasser, Essen und einen Rucksack suchen. Die ersten Quests schenken dir Verband, Rucksack und Werkzeug.

3 · Zum Handelsposten

Lauf zum Handelsposten Green Mountain (Schutzzone). Dort: Quest Board, Peter, Händler, Bank/ATM, Garage.

4 · Erste Coins

Nimm Quest 1 „Ankunft bei R3DACTED“ am Quest Board an, lauf zu Steves Außenposten im Westen und arbeite die Ketten ab. Gefundenes Loot verkaufst du an Naomi, Maria und Quinn.

5 · Ausrüsten

Kaufe Rucksack, Wasser, Verbände und Munition. Alles zu Preisen findest du in Beispielpreise und Verkaufen erklärt.

6 · Ziele setzen

Einsteiger-Fahrzeug in der Garage, Basis bauen, Jagd & Angeln, später Season Pass und Boss.

Your first 30 minutes

1 · Pick a spawn

After joining you see the spawn map: Coast (random) always works, the 8 towns (Cherno, Elektro, Kamyshovo, Solnechny, Orlovets, Nizhnee, Berezino, Svetlojarsk) are then locked for 10 minutes.

2 · Survive

Find water, food and a backpack. The first quests give you bandages, a backpack and tools.

3 · Go to the trading post

Walk to the Green Mountain trading post (safe zone). There: quest board, Peter, traders, bank/ATM, garage.

4 · First coins

Accept quest 1 "Arrival at R3DACTED" at the quest board, walk to Steve's outpost in the west and work through the chains. Sell found loot to Naomi, Maria and Quinn.

5 · Gear up

Buy a backpack, water, bandages and ammo. Prices: see Example prices and How selling works.

6 · Set goals

Starter vehicle at the garage, build a base, hunting & fishing, later Season Pass and boss.
