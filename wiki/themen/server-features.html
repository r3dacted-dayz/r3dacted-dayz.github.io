Das bietet R3DACTED

🛒 Handel & Geld

Händler mit R3D Coins, Schwarzmarkt, Spielermarkt (Auktion), Bank/ATM, Wechsel-Automat.

📜 Quests & Season Pass

98 Quests (Quest Board, Peter, Steve) und ein Kosmetik-Season-Pass im Hauptmenü.

🚗 Fahrzeuge

Garage mit Schlüsseln (MuchCarKey) und AutoCarFlip – umgekippte Fahrzeuge wieder aufstellen.

🪂 Events

Airdrops (Funkspruch, PvP-Zone), Boss, KI-Söldner-Camps.

🏗️ Bauen & Lagern

BaseBuildingPlus, CodeLock, APH-Lager, RaG-Möbel, Werkbank-Kit (zm Workbench), Skin-System (Spraydosen).

🌱 Natur & Küche

Jagen, Angeln, Gärtnern (AgricultureCore, GardeningPlus), Kochen mit CookZ.

🧪 Crafting

CraftingPlus mit Stationen; optional die Drogen-Ketten (Mod iM7s Drug Running).

🎖️ Komfort

Achievements, Killfeed, HoverLoot, Gruppen & Karten-Marker.

📻 Extras

Radio (Psyerns Radio Show), Bitcoin-Mining-Farm, NomNom-Sammelkarten, Dogtags, Tombstone.

🔫 Waffen & Gear

Waffenpakete (Delta Force Weapons, Buca) und Ausrüstung (FOG, BallerZ, RaG).

🧟 Gegner

Horden (Dark Horde), Mutanten und KI (SFP), Expansion-AI.

What R3DACTED offers

🛒 Trade & money

Traders with R3D coins, Black Market, player market (auction), bank/ATM, exchange machine.

📜 Quests & Season Pass

98 quests (Quest Board, Peter, Steve) and a cosmetic Season Pass in the main menu.

🚗 Vehicles

Garage with keys (MuchCarKey) and AutoCarFlip – set flipped vehicles upright again.

🪂 Events

Airdrops (radio call, PvP zone), boss, AI mercenary camps.

🏗️ Building & storage

BaseBuildingPlus, CodeLock, APH storage, RaG furniture, workbench kit (zm Workbench), skin system (spray cans).

🌱 Nature & kitchen

Hunting, fishing, gardening (AgricultureCore, GardeningPlus), cooking with CookZ.

🧪 Crafting

CraftingPlus with stations; optionally the drug chains (iM7s Drug Running mod).

🎖️ Comfort

Achievements, killfeed, HoverLoot, groups & map markers.

📻 Extras

Radio (Psyerns Radio Show), Bitcoin mining farm, NomNom collectibles, dogtags, tombstone.

🔫 Weapons & gear

Weapon packs (Delta Force Weapons, Buca) and gear (FOG, BallerZ, RaG).

🧟 Enemies

Hordes (Dark Horde), mutants and AI (SFP), Expansion AI.
